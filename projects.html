My Projects

Nom Roller

Prototyped Nom Roller, a food decision app built in just a few days for Astra | Young Builders Symposium (Toronto Tech Week).

  • Problem

    Choosing what to eat is often a frustrating, indecisive process. We wanted a fun, lightweight tool to simplify the decision.

  • Method / Process

    I led the design direction by sketching low-fidelity concepts, building interactive flows in Figma, and aligning the team of four around a cohesive vision.

  • Solution

    My team and I created a modern, minimal interface that used randomized food suggestions, bold red hunger cues, and clean button layouts to keep the experience simple and engaging.

  • Validation / Measurement

    We gathered quick user feedback through forms and live testing at the event, refining interaction patterns and visual clarity based on real responses.

  • Iteration

    After reviewing user feedback, my team and I discussed what we might have done differently in terms of pacing, button design, and overall layout to balance usability.
